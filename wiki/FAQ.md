# FAQ

## My Pokémon isn't doing anything

Check these in order:

1. **Can it work?** It has to be yours, out in the world (sent out or in a pasture), have a work
   skill, and have its work switched on (the interact wheel's bottom slot). See
   [Workers](Workers#who-works).
2. **Can it do the job?** Look it up on [Work skills by Pokémon](Work-Skills-by-Pokemon). A Water
   type won't light a furnace.
3. **Is the job close enough?** Workers look 32 blocks out by default, and a party Pokémon only
   works within 24 blocks of you. See [Configuration](Configuration).
4. **Is the job actually available?** For example, a furnace needs smeltable items in it, farmland
   needs the space above it empty, and a Planting worker needs seeds in a chest.
5. **Can it reach the spot?** If there's no path, the worker skips that spot for up to 30 seconds.
6. **Is it hungry, Tired, unhappy or on strike?** Look over its head. See [Hunger](Hunger),
   [Sleep](Sleep) and [Mood](Mood).
7. **Is the job working yet?** Mining isn't, and nothing uses Handiwork, Medicine Production,
   Generating Electricity, Cooling or Farming yet. See [Jobs](Jobs).

## The harvest is left on the ground

Picking up drops needs the **Transporting** skill (Flying types). Grass types plant and harvest but
don't pick items up. Add a Flying worker to your farm, and a chest with room for it to fill.

## My transporter is carrying items around

It's trying to [deliver](Jobs#deliver-items) them but can't find a chest with room. It carries on
with its other jobs meanwhile. Place a chest with free space nearby.

## A worker chopped down something I built

Workers only chop a group of logs that has at least 4 natural leaf blocks near it, stands on the
ground, and touches nothing but logs, leaves, plants and ground. Leaves you placed don't count, and
a log touching planks, glass or any other building block rules it out. So builds are almost always
safe. One that was chopped was likely made only of logs, with natural leaves left touching it.

## Does it work on servers?

Yes. Palworks goes on the server and every player's game, along with Cobblemon.
