# Palworks Wiki

Palworks is a Minecraft mod for **1.21.1** (Fabric and NeoForge) that turns your
[Cobblemon](https://cobblemon.com) Pokémon into workers, Palworld-style. Send a Pokémon out and it
looks for work nearby and does it on its own: smelting, farming, watering, chopping trees, and
carrying items to your chests.

What a Pokémon can do depends on its **type** and, for some, its **species**: a Fire type lights
furnaces, a Grass type tends crops, a Water type waters them, a Flying type hauls items, and so on.
How well it does it depends on how strong it is.

> Palworks requires Cobblemon. It does nothing without it.

## Looking after workers

- **[Hunger](Hunger)**: work makes workers hungry; fill **troughs** and they eat from them.
- **[Sleep](Sleep)**: pastured workers sleep in **beds** at their species' hours, or wake Tired and nap in one by day.
- **[Mood](Mood)**: overwork, hunger and no rest wear their mood down, and an unhappy worker goes on
  strike.

## Project status

Palworks is in early development and isn't published to a mod host yet. Some jobs are fully
working and others are still being built. The [Jobs](Jobs) page shows the status of each one.

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an
